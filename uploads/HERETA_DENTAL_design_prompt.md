# HERETA DENTAL — Design Prompt for Claude Design / Block Builder

---

## BRIEF

Design a complete, production-ready website for **HERETA DENTAL** — an expert dental clinic in Lviv, Ukraine. The site should feel elevated but approachable: not a cold medical institution, not a budget clinic — a place where a family genuinely wants to go. Think "boutique wellness meets clinical trust."

---

## BRAND IDENTITY

**Name:** HERETA DENTAL  
**Tagline:** Експертна стоматологія  
**Location:** вул. Шафарика, 1, Львів  
**Rating:** 5.0 / 5.0

**Visual character of the real clinic:**
- Interior: light, minimalist, warm wooden accents, clean white walls
- Atmosphere: calm, premium but not intimidating, family-friendly
- Details: branded coffee machine in waiting area, unicorn decor in kids' room, patients receive branded gift bags after appointments
- Doctors are personable and run educational video content (truth-or-myth format)

**Palette to derive from:**
- Dominant: warm white / off-white (#FAFAF8 or similar)
- Secondary: warm sand / linen tone (#EDE8DF or similar)
- Accent: one soft premium tone — choose between sage green, dusty rose, or warm terracotta — pick the one that best matches the clinic's warmth and trust positioning. Do NOT use cold blue or generic "medical teal."
- Text: near-black with warmth (#1C1A18 or similar), not pure #000000
- Wood/natural: a muted warm brown for dividers or subtle texture references

**Typography:**
- Display / headings: a refined serif (e.g. Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display, or similar) — conveys expertise, trust, elegance
- Body: a clean modern sans-serif (e.g. Inter, DM Sans) — readable, approachable
- Scale: generous. Large hero headline, clear hierarchy. Generous line-height throughout.

## SITE STRUCTURE — SECTIONS TO BUILD

Design all sections as a single-page website with smooth scroll. The order and exact layout of each section is yours to decide — use your judgment as a designer. Below is the content map; you determine how to present it visually.

### 1. HERO
Goal: Immediately establish trust and warmth. Not a generic "we care about your smile" banner.
Content:
- Headline: something like "Стоматологія, якій довіряють сім'ї" or "Де закінчується страх — починається здоров'я"
- Subline: Експертна стоматологія у Львові
- CTA button: "Записатися" (links to Instagram DM or phone)
- Background: full-width — either a warm photo of clinic interior or an elegant abstract background using brand palette. No stock smile photos.
- Optional: subtle animated entrance for headline text

### 2. ABOUT / POSITIONING
Goal: Build immediate trust. Speak human, not corporate.
Content:
- Short clinic philosophy statement (2–3 sentences)
- 4 key values in visual tiles or icons:
  - Безболісне лікування
  - Сучасне обладнання
  - Турбота про всю сім'ю
  - Стерильність та безпека
- Do NOT use generic checkmark lists. Think visual — cards, icons, small illustrations, or typographic emphasis.

### 3. SERVICES
Goal: Make it scannable. Patients should find their need in under 5 seconds.
Content — 5 categories, each with sub-items:
1. Терапія та профілактика (caries treatment, fillings, hygiene)
2. Ортопедія та ендодонтія (root canals, prosthetics)
3. Хірургія (extractions, surgical procedures)
4. Естетична стоматологія (whitening, veneers, restoration)
5. Дитяча стоматологія (separate kids' room, nitrous oxide sedation)

Design suggestion: use an accordion, tabbed layout, or elegant card grid. Each category should feel like its own "department" with a brief description, not just a bullet list.

### 4. WHY HERETA DENTAL (Differentiators)
Goal: Set the clinic apart from competitors visually and emotionally.
Content — unique features:
- Окремий дитячий кабінет (unicorn decor, friendly atmosphere)
- Власний стерилізаційний кабінет
- Лазерне відбілювання — Biolaser Flux Plus
- Sedation for anxious children (закис азоту)
- Кавова зона для пацієнтів (Catler branded coffee machine)
- Рейтинг 5.0/5.0 на doc.ua та kamon.ua
- Освітній відео-контент від лікарів

Design: avoid a plain feature grid. Consider a "before/after" narrative, or a bold typographic statement like "Немає страху. Є результат." combined with feature callouts.

### 5. KIDS' SECTION
Goal: Address parents directly. Reduce fear about bringing children to the dentist.
Content:
- Emphasize the separate kids' room with friendly atmosphere
- Mention nitrous oxide sedation — explain it's safe and calming
- Tone: warm, reassuring, not clinical
- Consider a visual that feels playful but still trustworthy

### 6. TEAM / DOCTORS
Goal: Put faces to the expertise. Trust is personal.
Content:
- Section title: "Наші лікарі" or "Команда, якій довіряєш"
- Placeholder for doctor cards (photo + name + specialization)
- Note that doctors run educational video content — could link to Instagram
- Design: clean doctor cards, warm tones, not cold corporate headshots layout

### 7. REVIEWS / SOCIAL PROOF
Goal: Let patients speak.
Content:
- Rating badges: 5.0 / 5.0 — doc.ua, kamon.ua
- 3–4 patient review cards (placeholder text in Ukrainian)
- Mention of video reviews (link to Instagram)
- Design: elegant, not a typical "testimonials carousel." Consider a large pull-quote treatment combined with star rating badges.

### 8. CLINIC GALLERY / ATMOSPHERE
Goal: Show the space — make people want to walk in.
Content:
- Grid or masonry gallery of clinic interior photos
- Caption note about comfort, warmth, modern equipment
- Could include the coffee zone, sterilization room, kids' room, treatment room

### 9. CONTACT / BOOKING CTA
Goal: Zero friction to book an appointment.
Content:
- Address: вул. Шафарика, 1, Львів, 79000 (Личаківський район)
- Hours: Пн–Пт 10:00–20:00 / Сб 10:00–15:00 / Нд — вихідний
- How to book: Direct в Instagram / за телефоном
- Amenities: Паркінг · Дитяча кімната · Оплата карткою · Wi-Fi
- CTA button: "Записатися в Instagram"

### 10. FOOTER

## DESIGN DIRECTION & RULES

**Tone:** Premium warmth. Not clinical white + blue. Not luxury-cold. Think: a high-end café that happens to be a dental clinic.

**Layout philosophy:**
- Generous whitespace. Let content breathe.
- Sections alternate between white and linen/sand backgrounds to create rhythm without harsh breaks.
- One bold typographic moment per section — don't let any section feel like a template.

**Motion & Interaction:**
- Smooth scroll-triggered fade-ins for key sections
- Subtle hover states on cards and buttons
- No excessive animation — restraint is the signature here
- Consider one ambient visual moment: perhaps a slow-breathing hero background or a gentle parallax on the hero image

**Imagery:**
- Use warm-toned placeholder images or gradients if no photos available
- Avoid blue-toned stock medical photos
- The aesthetic reference: Scandinavian dental/wellness clinics, not Ukrainian generic clinic sites

**Mobile:**
- Fully responsive. Hero must work on 375px.
- Navigation: sticky header with hamburger on mobile
- Touch-friendly CTAs (min 44px tap targets)

**Signature element (the one thing to be remembered by):**
A large, serif typographic "trust statement" — one bold sentence in Ukrainian, displayed at oversized scale (like a quote wall) somewhere mid-page. Something like:
> *"Без болю. Без страху. Тільки результат."*
This should feel like the clinic's philosophy made visual — not a button, not a CTA, just a statement of identity.

---

## CONTENT — FINAL COPY NOTES

All copy should be in Ukrainian. Tone: warm, confident, human. Not corporate. Not "we are committed to providing quality services." More like: "Ваш зуб болить — ми знаємо, що робити. І зробимо так, щоб наступного разу ви прийшли не зі страхом, а з посмішкою."

Keep headlines short and direct. Use the service content exactly as provided in the content document. Do not invent services or claims.

## TECHNICAL OUTPUT

Deliver as a single self-contained HTML file with embedded CSS and JS. No external frameworks required except Google Fonts. All styles inline or in `<style>` block. Fully functional static site. Include smooth scroll behavior, mobile hamburger menu, and any micro-interactions in vanilla JS.
